02 — Problem Statement
What We're
Solving

The Core Challenge

In India, access to timely healthcare remains a major challenge, especially in rural and underserved regions where medical infrastructure is limited. Millions of people do not have easy access to qualified doctors, diagnostic facilities, or affordable treatment options. As a result, diseases such as Tuberculosis, Pneumonia, Malaria, and skin infections are often detected at later stages, significantly increasing both health risks and treatment costs.

A major issue is the delay in early diagnosis. People either ignore initial symptoms or lack the resources to get proper medical attention at the right time. In many cases, by the time diagnosis happens, the condition has already worsened, making treatment more complex and expensive.

Traditional diagnosis relies heavily on hospital visits, laboratory tests, and specialized equipment, which are not only costly but also time-consuming. This creates a barrier for people who need quick and accessible health screening.

Additionally, existing digital healthcare solutions are fragmented, with each system focusing on only one type of input such as symptoms, images, or audio. This lack of integration often leads to incomplete analysis and reduces the overall accuracy of predictions.

These challenges highlight a critical gap in accessible, affordable, and early disease detection, creating a strong need for a unified, intelligent, and user-friendly healthcare solution.

Innovation

HELPix introduces a multimodal AI-based healthcare system that integrates cough sound analysis, skin image detection, and symptom-based prediction into a single platform, improving accuracy and usability.

Doctor Booking

HELPix allows users to book doctor consultations directly through the application, making healthcare more accessible, faster, and convenient without the need for physical visits.

Smart Health Monitoring

The system can integrate with smart wearable devices (such as smartwatches) to monitor real-time health data like heart rate, activity level, and daily health patterns.

Data Source

Uses Coswara (cough audio), skin cancer image datasets (HAM10000, ISIC 2024, BCN 20000), and structured symptom-based data for training deep learning models.

09 — Research Publication
Scientific
Foundations

Reliability-Oriented Skin-Lesion Classification Using HELPix-R

Our research focuses on building a highly reliable deep-learning framework (HELPix-R) that goes beyond simple accuracy. We emphasize calibration, explainability, uncertainty estimation, and rigorous external testing to address the critical gaps between lab performance and real-world clinical use. To date, the system has been successfully tested on over 500 individuals in real-world clinical scenarios.

Rigorous Data Auditing

To prevent data leakage, an extensive duplicate audit using SHA-256 and perceptual hashing was performed across large repositories (ISIC 2024, HAM10000, and BCN20000). This ensured strict independence between training, validation, and testing splits.

Performance vs. Transportability

HELPix-R demonstrated exceptional internal discrimination with an AUROC of 0.9778 (97.78%). However, independent testing on smartphone-captured images (PAD-UFES-20) revealed performance degradation, highlighting the real-world challenge of domain shift and transportability in medical AI.

Explainable AI (XAI)

We utilized Grad-CAM for visual attribution to see which lesion regions influenced the model's predictions. A quantitative deletion experiment verified that masking these salient regions effectively reduced malignancy probabilities, confirming attribution sensitivity.

Calibration & Uncertainty

High accuracy isn't enough if confidence scores are distorted. We applied Temperature Scaling to calibrate probabilities and Monte Carlo Dropout to estimate predictive uncertainty, ensuring the model's outputs are highly reliable.
